# Open learning labs

Interactive, beginner-friendly websites for transportation engineering: each one has animations, simulators, quizzes, mind maps, study guides and slides, and most have runnable code.

## 🎓 Oral Exams: Transportation and Pavement Concepts

61 interactive, beginner-friendly pages for PhD comprehensive oral exam preparation, covering traffic flow theory, urban transportation planning, transportation safety, transportation data analysis and pavement engineering. Each page has sliders, "Try this" experiments, a worked example and a follow-up question.

**[Open site](https://smrahmanbd.github.io/Oral-Exams/)** · **[Repository](https://github.com/smrahmanbd/Oral-Exams)**

## 🚥 CE677 Traffic Control and Operation

Four modules on adaptive traffic signal control, from established systems to learning and agentic controllers. Course hub: [ce677-traffic-control-and-operation](https://github.com/smrahmanbd/ce677-traffic-control-and-operation).

| Module | What it covers | Website | Repository |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SCATS** | The Sydney Coordinated Adaptive Traffic System in three levels, with a SCATS-style controller for SUMO | [Open site](https://smrahmanbd.github.io/scats-learning-lab/) | [scats-learning-lab](https://github.com/smrahmanbd/scats-learning-lab) |
| **SCOOT** | The Split Cycle Offset Optimisation Technique in three levels, plus a design workshop (designing SCOOT for a town) and a SCOOT-style controller for SUMO | [Open site](https://smrahmanbd.github.io/scoot-learning-lab/) | [scoot-learning-lab](https://github.com/smrahmanbd/scoot-learning-lab) |
| **Reinforcement Learning** | A signal controller that learns from trial, error and reward, in two levels: a Foundation lesson with a live junction, a Q-table and a neural network, and a Research level with landmark methods, gaps and eight research ideas | [Open site](https://smrahmanbd.github.io/rl-atsc-lab/) | [rl-atsc-lab](https://github.com/smrahmanbd/rl-atsc-lab) |
| **Agentic AI** | AI agents that perceive, reason, act through a safety shield and coordinate with neighbours and connected vehicles; three Goes from principles to SUMO labs to open research questions | [Open site](https://smrahmanbd.github.io/agentic-atsc-lab/) | [agentic-atsc-lab](https://github.com/smrahmanbd/agentic-atsc-lab) |
